Small AI models, running in your browser

Make images and summarize text and documents on your own device. The models download once from this site and run locally, on your GPU or CPU: your prompts, text and results never leave the browser.

Generate images

Describe a picture and get a 512×512 image, or make 16×16 pixel-art sprites.

  • Small SD: photos and illustrations, on the GPU (WebGPU or WebNN) or CPU
  • Mini Pixel Art: game-style sprites with transparent backgrounds

Summarize text

Paste an article or report and get a short summary in a few seconds.

  • DistilBART, a model trained to summarize news: Fast (290 MB) or Best (1.1 GB)
  • Runs on the CPU in a background thread; long text is split into parts

Summarize a file

Pick a PDF, Word document or text file and get a summary of it.

  • The file is read in your browser and never uploaded, not even to this site
  • Same summarizer and options as Summarize text; you can edit the text first
